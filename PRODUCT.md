# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Technical Operations specialists and on-call engineers investigating payment-provider incidents under time pressure. Their primary job is to understand what degraded, assess customer impact, and choose a safe next action without interpreting raw telemetry from scratch.

## Product Purpose

Turn normalized payment-provider telemetry into explainable, auditable incident intelligence. Success means an operator can move from alert to evidence, probable causes, and reversible recommendations quickly while retaining human control over mitigation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should have the operational density and clarity associated with Linear and Datadog without copying either product.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble neon cyberpunk monitoring software, a generic SaaS wall of identical cards, or a visual clone of Grafana. Avoid decorative AI imagery, opaque automation claims, and interfaces that imply the model can make production changes autonomously.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before explanation: keep the measured signals adjacent to every AI conclusion.
2. Calm under pressure: establish hierarchy through structure and language, not visual alarm noise.
3. Human authority is explicit: recommendations remain advisory and status changes are auditable.
4. Progressive depth: make the incident state scannable first and investigation detail available without navigation churn.
5. Operational honesty: distinguish deterministic catalog decisions, live model analysis, and analysis-unavailable states clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, and status communication through labels and shape in addition to color.
